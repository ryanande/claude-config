### Patterns

Both readable meetings share a shape worth naming: **CRM is treated as the system of record, and cross-boundary coordination is done by replicating data into a local table and polling/querying it — not by messaging.** In Device Ordering it's a replicated device-orders table to pre-empt a hidden CRM sync error; in the Data synch it's Function-App table-polling (`NISTA recon status`, `batch files created`) with an explicit "no NServiceBus." These are pre-ONYX habits solidifying in the tolls/device domain right as G2's messaging mandate and back-office-containment priorities are supposed to take hold. Neither is wrong for today, but the accumulation is the thing to watch — each replicated-table-instead-of-event is a future migration cost.
